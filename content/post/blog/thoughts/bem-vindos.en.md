These days we spend so much time taking in information that we rarely stop to make sense of our own thoughts. This blog is my way of sharing how I see the world. It's often a simple, everyday take, with no expert credentials behind it, but it might still mean something to someone.
:::

As I write this, I've got one eye on the blog editor and the other on Estudiantes vs. Flamengo, rooting against Flamengo with everything I've got, like any good Palmeiras fan would.

That pretty much sums me up: 90% of the time I'm thinking about the future, about new projects and ideas, and about how to give my family a better life. The other 10% goes to dreaming about Palmeiras winning everything. That's life!

I hope this turns into something worthwhile, and that one day the people who care about me can always find a bit of who I am here. I'm not always a ray of sunshine, but I always try to be kind, humble and happy. Avanti Palestra!
